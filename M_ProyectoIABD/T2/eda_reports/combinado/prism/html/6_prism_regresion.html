Residuals

What the diagnostic probe left over. Everything here describes that fit on this data — it is model-conditional, not a property of the dataset.

Residual against predicted value

A trustworthy fit straddles the zero rule evenly at every prediction. The band is the local spread: where it fans out, one prediction interval will not hold.

Average error by predicted range

Bars above the rule are ranges the model under-predicts; below, ranges it over-predicts. A fit can average out to zero error and still be wrong at both ends.
